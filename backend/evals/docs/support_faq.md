# Brightly Smart Thermostat - Support FAQ

## Setup
The Brightly thermostat works with 24 volt heating and cooling systems. It does not work with line voltage systems such as electric baseboard heaters. The Brightly app is available for iPhone (iOS 15 or later) and Android (version 9 or later). Setup takes about 20 minutes. Turn off power at your breaker before removing the old thermostat.

## Wi-Fi problems
Brightly connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only. It does not connect to 5 GHz networks. If the screen shows error E12, the thermostat cannot reach your router: move closer to the router or restart your router. If it shows error E40, the C wire (common wire) is missing or loose, and you need to check the wiring or install the included power adapter.

## Factory reset
To factory reset the thermostat, press and hold the ring for 10 seconds until the screen turns blue, then release. Resetting erases your schedules and Wi-Fi settings but keeps your account.

## Battery
The thermostat has a built-in backup battery that keeps the clock and schedules for up to 8 hours during a power cut. The battery is not replaceable.

## Warranty and returns
Brightly has a 2 year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover water damage or damage from wrong wiring. You can return an unopened or used thermostat within 60 days for a full refund. Return shipping is free in the United States. We ship to the United States and Canada only.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days and is free on orders over 75 dollars. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs 14 dollars. Orders placed before noon Central Time ship the same day.

## Contacting support
Phone support is open Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm Central Time, at 1-800-555-0142. Live chat in the app is open every day from 7 am to 10 pm Central Time. Email replies take up to 24 hours. Weekend phone support is not available.

## Energy features
Away mode lowers energy use when no one is home, using your phone's location. Brightly works with Alexa and Google Home. It does not work with Apple HomeKit.
