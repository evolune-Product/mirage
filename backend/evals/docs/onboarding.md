# Harbor Logistics - New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Harbor Logistics. This guide covers your first weeks.

## Day one
Your first day starts at 9:30 am at the Denver headquarters, 400 Larimer Street, 5th floor. Ask for Priya in the front lobby. Bring a government ID and your signed offer letter for the I-9 form. Your laptop is handed to you by the IT desk, and you will get your badge by 11 am. Lunch on the first day is provided by your team.

## Accounts and tools
IT creates your email and Slack accounts before you arrive. You must set up two-factor authentication on the first day. We use Slack for chat, Jira for tickets, and Google Workspace for email and documents. For password problems, contact the IT help desk at helpdesk@harbor.example or press extension 4400.

## Benefits
Health insurance, dental, and vision coverage start on the first day of the month after your start date. You must enroll within 30 days of starting, or you will wait until the next open enrollment in November. The company matches 401(k) contributions up to 4 percent after 90 days of employment.

## Time off
Full-time employees get 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, plus 10 company holidays. PTO accrues monthly. Unused PTO carries over up to 5 days into the next year. Sick days are separate: 6 per year. Request time off in the Workday app, and your manager approves it.

## Remote work
Most roles are hybrid: 3 days in the office (Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday) and 2 days remote. Fully remote work needs written approval from a vice president. A 150 dollar one-time home office stipend is available after your first 30 days.

## Expenses and travel
Submit expenses in Workday within 30 days. Flights must be booked through the travel portal at least 14 days ahead. Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day.

## Probation and reviews
The first 90 days are an introductory period. You will have check-ins with your manager at 30, 60, and 90 days. The first formal performance review happens after 6 months. Payroll runs on the 15th and the last day of each month.
