# Riverside Family Clinic - Patient Information

## Hours and location
Riverside Family Clinic is at 82 Mill Road, Springfield. We are open Monday to Thursday 8 am to 5 pm, Friday 8 am to 1 pm, and closed on weekends. The after-hours nurse line is 555-0199 and is answered every day from 5 pm to 10 pm.

## New patients
New patients should arrive 15 minutes early and bring a photo ID, their insurance card, and a list of current medications. You can fill out the new patient forms online through the patient portal before your visit. The first visit for a new adult patient lasts 40 minutes. Children's first visits last 30 minutes.

## Insurance and payment
We accept Blue Cross, Aetna, Cigna, and Medicare. We do not accept Medicaid at this time. The standard copay for a primary care visit is 25 dollars, payable at check-in. Patients without insurance pay a flat 90 dollars for a standard visit. We accept cards and cash but not checks.

## Appointments
Book by phone at 555-0123 or through the patient portal. Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your visit. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged a 35 dollar fee. Same-day sick visits are usually available if you call before 10 am.

## Lab work and fasting
Routine blood tests are done on site. For cholesterol and glucose tests you must fast for 8 hours before the draw, and you may drink plain water. Lab results are posted to the patient portal within 3 business days.

## Prescriptions
Refill requests should be made through the portal or your pharmacy, and are processed within 2 business days. We do not prescribe controlled substances by telehealth. Prescriptions for controlled substances require an in-person visit.

## Telehealth
Video visits are available for follow-ups, medication reviews, and minor illnesses, Monday to Thursday. A video visit costs a 15 dollar copay. For chest pain, trouble breathing, or any emergency, call 911 instead of the clinic.

## Vaccines
Flu shots are available from October through March, with no appointment needed during opening hours. School and travel vaccines require an appointment.
