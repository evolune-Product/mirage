# Northwind Analytics - Pricing

Northwind Analytics is a dashboard and reporting tool for small online shops. There are three paid plans and one free trial.

## Plans
The Starter plan costs 29 dollars per month and includes 3 user seats, 5 dashboards, and 30 days of data history.
The Pro plan costs 99 dollars per month and includes 10 user seats, unlimited dashboards, 12 months of data history, and scheduled email reports.
The Business plan costs 299 dollars per month and includes 40 user seats, unlimited dashboards, unlimited data history, single sign-on (SSO), and a dedicated customer success manager.

## Billing
Paying yearly instead of monthly gives a 20 percent discount on every plan. Invoices are sent by email on the first day of each billing period. We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. We do not accept PayPal or cryptocurrency. Bank transfer is available only on the Business plan, for annual billing.

## Free trial
Every new account gets a 14 day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card is needed to start the trial. When the trial ends the account is moved to a read-only mode until you pick a plan.

## Extra seats and overage
Extra user seats cost 8 dollars per seat per month on Starter and Pro, and 6 dollars per seat per month on Business. Data connectors are included in all plans, but each plan allows up to 100,000 tracked orders per month. Above that limit, each additional 10,000 orders cost 15 dollars.

## Refunds and cancellation
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and are not refunded for the current month. Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days there are no refunds on yearly plans. To cancel, go to Settings, then Billing, then Cancel plan.

## Discounts
Registered non-profit organizations get 25 percent off any plan. Contact sales at sales@northwind.example with proof of status. Northwind Analytics does not currently offer student discounts or lifetime deals.

## Sales and support
The sales team answers questions Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm Eastern Time. Annual contracts above 10,000 dollars can include custom terms.
